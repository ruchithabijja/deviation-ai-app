Deviation Report

Site: API Manufacturing Unit
Date of Occurrence: 26 September 2026
Source: Warehouse
Product: Amoxicillin API
Batch Number: AMX-260926-04

During dispensing of raw material, the operator noticed that the material label showed a different lot number from the lot number recorded in the dispensing document.

The material was not used in manufacturing. It was immediately segregated and placed under hold pending verification by Quality Assurance.

No product was manufactured using the affected material.
