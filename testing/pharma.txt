Deviation Report

Site: API Manufacturing Unit
Date of Occurrence: 27 September 2026
Source: Production
Product: Paracetamol API
Batch Number: PCM-260927-01

During the manufacturing process of Paracetamol API, the reactor temperature increased to 96°C. The approved operating temperature range for the process is 80°C to 90°C.

The deviation was detected by the production operator during routine monitoring. The batch was immediately placed on hold and the production supervisor was informed.

There was a potential impact on product quality because the temperature exceeded the approved process range. Quality Assurance was notified for further investigation.
